Privacy Policy

Bethaniya Ministries mobile app · Last updated: 16 September 2026

This is a provisional privacy notice and has not yet been reviewed by legal counsel. It accurately describes what the app does today. The church operating this app should have it reviewed before wide public launch.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Bethaniya Ministries Android application (package com.bethaniyaministries.app), operated by Bethaniya Ministries.

What we collect

You can use the app only after creating an account. Two sign-in methods are offered:

  • Email address and password. Firebase Authentication stores your email address and a securely hashed password. We never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Google Sign-In. Google provides your email address and, if your Google profile has one, your name and profile picture.

Alongside your account we store a profile document containing: your display name, your profile photo if you upload one, your language and theme preferences, whether you have enabled notifications, and your role in the app (ordinary member by default).

Content you create in the app — for example private prayer requests, or your progress through a reading plan — is stored against your account.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, messages, call logs, camera roll (beyond a photo you deliberately choose to upload), or microphone.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers or any advertising SDK.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

Solely to operate the app: to sign you in, to show you your own profile and content, to let church administrators publish announcements, events, songs and reading material, and to remember your preferences.

Who can see your information

Church administrators can see your name, email address and role, as needed to run the app. Database security rules restrict what each administrator role can read or change, and restrict your own private content to your account alone.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Service providers

The app runs on Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes and stores this data on our behalf under Google's own privacy terms. Signing in with Google involves Google as an identity provider. Event video links may open YouTube, which is governed by Google's privacy policy once you leave this app.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and profile for as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact the church using the support email address shown in the app's Settings screen. We will action the request within a reasonable period.

Children

This app is intended for a general church audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your data, contact Bethaniya Ministries using the support email address shown in the app's Settings screen.
